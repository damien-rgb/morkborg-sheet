When you get better

Roll all of this in the dice tray, one die at a time.

  1. More hit points. Roll 6d10. Equal to your maximum or higher, add a d6 to the maximum.
  2. Every ability. Roll a d6 against each. Equal or higher, up by 1. Lower, down by 1. Never above +6 or below -3. Anything from -3 to +1 always goes up, unless the d6 shows a 1.
  3. In the debris. Roll a d6: 1 to 3 nothing, 4 is 3d10 silver, 5 an unclean scroll, 6 a sacred scroll.
