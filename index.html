When you get better

Roll all of this in the dice tray, one die at a time.

  1. More hit points. Roll 6d10. If it is the same as your maximum hit points or higher, add a d6 to the maximum.
  2. Every ability changes. Roll a d6 against each one. The same or higher, it goes up by 1. Lower, it goes down by 1. Never above +6 or below -3. An ability between -3 and +1 always goes up by 1, unless the d6 shows a 1.
  3. Left in the debris. Roll a d6. On 1 to 3 nothing, on 4 you find 3d10 silver, on 5 an unclean scroll, on 6 a sacred scroll.
